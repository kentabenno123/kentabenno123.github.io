Privacy policy

Mindful Player for Android and iOS, and this website

In short

Mindful Player does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools and sends nothing to me or to anyone else. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Controller

Robin Helm
Email: mail@robinhelm.de

What the app stores on your device

  • which audio file is on which button, and your own button names,
  • the folder you shared with the app, as an access permission of the operating system,
  • copies of audio files you choose from a network drive or the cloud, so they play offline. When no button uses a copy any more, the app deletes it automatically after one hour. Files that are already on your device are played directly and never copied.

This data lives in the app's private storage and is removed when you delete the app. On Android the app is excluded from cloud backup. On iOS your button setup may be part of your own device backup (iCloud or computer); copied audio files are excluded from it. I have no access to any of this data.

Permissions

  • Folder access: the app only reads the folder you choose. Credentials for network drives are handled by your operating system or by the app you used to connect the drive; Mindful Player never sees them.
  • Notifications (Android): show that a meditation is playing. The lock screen only shows “Meditation playing”, not the title.
  • Background playback: so the meditation keeps playing with the screen locked.
  • Internet: the Android app has no internet permission. The iOS app does not use the internet.

App stores and crash reports

When you download the app from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple process data under their own privacy policies. If you agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Google or Apple provide me with aggregated statistics and anonymous crash reports. They do not let me identify anyone.

This website

This website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and to protect against abuse. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other servers, not even fonts.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As the app sends no data to me, I usually hold no data about you. Feel free to write to mail@robinhelm.de anyway. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
