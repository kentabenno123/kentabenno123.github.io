Mindful Player

Open. Tap. Meditate.

How it works

  1. Press and hold a button and pick an audio file from your folder: on your device, in the cloud or on a network drive.
  2. Tap to play, tap again to pause, double tap to stop.
  3. Playback continues when the screen is off or your phone is locked.
  4. Use “Edit button” to give a button its own name or to clear it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my network drive (NAS)?

iPhone: In the Files app, tap “…” at the top right, choose “Connect to Server” and enter your drive's address (for example smb://192.168.1.20). It then shows up in Mindful Player under “Choose folder”.

Android: Android needs a small helper app for this, for example the free “CIFS Documents Provider”. Add your drive there; it then shows up in Mindful Player under “Choose folder”.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Files on your device play directly. Files from a network drive or the cloud are loaded once and then play offline. If you replace a loaded file, it stays for one more hour and is then deleted automatically. The app never deletes your own files.

Why does the app ask for notifications? (Android)

While a meditation plays, Android shows a notification. That is what allows playback to continue with the screen locked.

What does the app cost?

Nothing. No ads, no subscription, and it stays that way.

Which languages are available?

36 languages. The app follows your phone's language automatically.
